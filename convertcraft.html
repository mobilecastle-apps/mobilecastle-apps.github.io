Privacy Policy for ConvertCraft

App: ConvertCraft: Unit Converter Package name: com.mobilecastle.convertcraft Publisher: Mobile Castle Effective date: 22 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the ConvertCraft app (“ConvertCraft”, “the app”, “we”, “us”) does and does not handle, and how. Please read it together with the policies of the third parties named below.

By using ConvertCraft you agree to the practices described here.

Summary (plain language)

  • ConvertCraft has no user accounts and no sign-up. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or any similar personal detail.
  • Every conversion runs on your phone. The values you type, the results, the units you choose, your favorites, presets, custom units and history stay on your phone. Mobile Castle runs no server that receives them.
  • No runtime permission. ConvertCraft never asks for your location, files, camera, microphone or contacts.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob, with a consent form where the law requires one.
  • No purchases in this version. ConvertCraft offers nothing to buy: it does not connect to Google Play Billing and does not ask for the billing permission. Features marked PRO say “Premium features are coming soon.”

The rest of this policy gives the detail behind that summary.

Information ConvertCraft itself collects

ConvertCraft does not collect personal data on any server of ours, because Mobile Castle does not run a server that receives your data. Specifically:

  • No account or identity data. There is no login.
  • No upload of your conversions. The values you type, the results, your unit choices, favorites, presets, custom units and history are not sent to us or to anyone else by ConvertCraft.
  • No analytics or crash reporting of our own. ConvertCraft does not embed an analytics, crash-reporting or tracking service of Mobile Castle. Google’s services described below are independent third parties with their own data handling.
  • No exchange rates or other downloads of its own in this version: every unit factor is built into the app.

Data that stays on your device

Some information is stored only on your phone so the app can work and remember your settings. It is not sent to Mobile Castle:

  • Your preferences: for example theme, language, significant digits, number format, and the converter you used last with its value.
  • Favorites, presets and custom units you create.
  • History: the last 50 conversions you copied, swapped or left on screen. Keep history in Settings turns recording off; Clear history deletes it.
  • Rewarded uses: an ad you chose to watch opens one specialist unit family until you close the app, for up to an hour (kept in memory only), and a reward that arrived but was not used yet is counted until it is.
  • A local diagnostic log: a technical log of app events and errors in the app’s own folder, to help find problems. It never contains a value you typed, a result, a unit choice or your history, and it is not uploaded.

Settings > Clear all local data deletes ConvertCraft’s settings, favorites, presets, custom units and history (it keeps Google’s consent record). Uninstalling ConvertCraft removes everything it stored.

Export as CSV happens only when you ask: it hands your visible history to the app you pick in Android’s share sheet (for example a file manager, an email app or online storage). That app’s own privacy policy applies to what you give it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

ConvertCraft is free and supported by ads served through Google AdMob, a Google service. Banners appear only below the results on the Convert tab and on the Categories, Favorites and History tabs, never in the unit picker, Settings, About or any sheet you are working in. There are no full-screen ads when you open the app or move between screens. You may choose to watch a rewarded ad to open one specialist unit family for up to an hour; this is always optional.

The ad requests ConvertCraft sends carry nothing from your conversions: no value, result, unit or history is ever passed to AdMob. According to Google, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location;
  • app interactions related to ads (for example ad views and taps, app launch);
  • diagnostic information (for example launch time, hang rate, energy use);
  • device identifiers: the Android advertising ID and the app set ID.

This data is encrypted in transit. Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), ConvertCraft uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Security & privacy > Ads on newer Android versions).

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure: https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure

Settings also has a More apps row that opens Mobile Castle’s page on Google Play; it is a link, not an ad.

Purchases

This version of ConvertCraft offers no in-app purchases. It does not connect to Google Play Billing, it asks for no billing permission, and no part of the app can start a payment. Unit families and features marked PRO say “Premium features are coming soon.” If purchases are added in a later version, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

App permissions

ConvertCraft requests these Android permissions:

  • Internet (INTERNET) and network state (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): for ads. Your conversions are not sent.
  • Added by Google’s libraries: the advertising ID and Android’s privacy-preserving ad APIs (AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS) for ads, and background-work permissions (WAKE_LOCK, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, FOREGROUND_SERVICE) that these libraries use for their own short tasks.

ConvertCraft asks for no location, storage, camera, microphone, contacts, phone or SMS permission, and no permission at run time.

Children

ConvertCraft is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the app in a way that raises a concern, please contact us using the details below.

Data retention and deletion

Data ConvertCraft stores (preferences, favorites, presets, custom units, history, the diagnostic log) stays on your phone until you remove it, with Settings > Clear all local data, Clear history, or by uninstalling the app.

Mobile Castle holds no copy of your data, so there is no server-side deletion to request from us. For data collected by Google’s services (for example advertising data), use the controls Google provides (see its policies above) and your phone’s Ads settings.

Security

Because ConvertCraft works on your own phone and Mobile Castle runs no server for your data, your conversions are not exposed on our infrastructure. No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure; while we aim to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.
