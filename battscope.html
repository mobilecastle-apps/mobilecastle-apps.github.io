BattScope — Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for BattScope

App: BattScope: Battery Monitor Package name: com.mobilecastle.battscope Publisher: Mobile Castle Effective date: 29 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the BattScope app (“BattScope”, “the app”, “we”, “us”) does and does not handle, and how. Please read it together with the policies of the third parties named below.

By using BattScope you agree to the practices described here.

Summary (plain language)

  • BattScope has no user accounts and no sign-up. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or any similar personal detail.
  • Your battery data stays on your device. Readings, recorded sessions, battery history and alerts are stored only on your phone or tablet; a CSV file leaves your device only when you share it. Mobile Castle does not operate any server that receives them.
  • BattScope reads only the battery information Android makes public to every app, and whether the screen is on. It does not read your location, contacts, files, messages or which apps you use.
  • Notifications are asked for only when you switch on an alert. A recording you start runs as a foreground service with its own notification. BattScope asks for no location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage, phone, SMS or usage-access permission.
  • BattScope shows ads through Google AdMob, a third party. To serve ads, AdMob may collect device information and an advertising identifier. Ads never receive your battery readings, sessions or the names you type.
  • No purchases in this version. BattScope offers nothing to buy: it does not connect to Google Play Billing, and it does not ask for the billing permission. Premium features are coming soon.

The rest of this policy gives the detail behind that summary.

Information BattScope itself collects

BattScope does not collect personal data on its own servers, because Mobile Castle does not run a server that receives your data. Specifically:

  • No account / identity data. There is no login, so we do not collect a name, email address, password, phone number, or profile.
  • No upload of your battery data. Readings, sessions, history, alerts and the names you type are not copied to us.
  • No analytics SDK operated by us. BattScope does not embed our own analytics, crash-reporting or tracking service. (Google AdMob and the other Google services described below are independent third parties with their own data handling.)

What the app reads on your device

BattScope reads Android’s public battery information: the battery level, charging status, power source (charger, USB, wireless or dock), current, voltage, temperature, the charge and energy counters, the charge cycle count (Android 14 and later), Android’s battery condition and Android’s own time-to-full estimate, where your device reports them. It also notes whether the screen is on, to tell drain with the screen on from drain with the screen off. Values your device does not report are shown as “not reported”.

The What this device reports page shows your device’s manufacturer, model and Android version. These are read from Android on the device; they are not sent anywhere.

Data that stays on your device

Some information is stored locally on your device so the app can work and remember your settings. This data is not transmitted to Mobile Castle, and it is excluded from Android’s cloud backup. It includes:

  • Recorded sessions: for each charging or discharge session you record, its start and end, how it ended, the samples taken (time, level, current, voltage, temperature, charge counter, status, power source and whether the screen was on), the summary figures, the charger and cable names you chose to type, and the device model, Android version and app version the session was recorded with. They are saved in a database in the app’s own private storage. This version shows your 10 newest sessions and the last 24 hours of history; older ones stay on the device and are never deleted on their own.
  • Battery history: a point about every 15 minutes while background history is on (and whenever the app is open), with the same kinds of values. Points older than 90 days are removed automatically.
  • Alerts: the alerts you switched on and their levels, and the most recent alerts shown in the app.
  • Your preferences: for example theme, language, temperature unit, the rated battery capacity you entered, the automatic-recording and background-history switches, how your device reports its current (learned from its own readings), and which comparison or export a rewarded ad unlocked.
  • Files you export: a CSV file is written to the app’s private cache so you can share it; earlier ones older than a day are deleted the next time you export.
  • A local diagnostic log: BattScope writes a technical log (app events and errors) to a file in the app’s own folder on your device, to help troubleshoot problems on real hardware. It never contains your battery measurements, the names you type, or any identifier, and it stays on your device unless you deliberately share it.

All of it is removed when you uninstall BattScope. Settings → Clear history removes your sessions and battery history, and Clear all local data also removes your settings. See Data retention and deletion below.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

BattScope is free and supported by ads served through Google AdMob, a Google service. Ads appear as banners on the dashboard, the session list, a finished session’s detail and the battery history, never while a recording runs, and never on the dashboard while it shows a battery warning. There are no full-screen ads when you open the app or move between screens.

The ad requests BattScope sends carry no content from the app: no battery reading, session, history or name you typed is ever passed to AdMob. To select and show ads, Google AdMob (and the Google Mobile Ads SDK included in the app) may collect and process information such as:

  • a device advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID),
  • device and network information (for example device type, operating-system version, approximate location inferred from your IP address, and similar technical data),
  • ad-interaction and app-usage data related to the ads shown,
  • crash and diagnostic information about the ads software itself.

Google uses this information to serve and measure ads and to help prevent fraud and abuse, in accordance with Google’s own policies. Where required by law (for example, for users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), BattScope uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to show a consent form before serving personalised ads, and no ad is requested until that choice is made; you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options. You may also choose to watch a short rewarded ad to unlock one charger comparison or one CSV export of a session; this is optional, the app says what the ad unlocks before it plays, and no rewarded ad is offered while a recording runs.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • AdMob / Google Ads policies: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

You can limit ad personalisation and reset your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads on most Android phones).

Purchases

This version of BattScope offers no in-app purchases. It does not connect to Google Play Billing, it asks for no billing permission, and no part of the app can start a payment. Some features are marked for a future Premium version; they show “Premium features are coming soon.” If purchases are added in a later version, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

App permissions and why they are needed

BattScope requests the following Android permissions. Each is used only for the feature described:

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, Android 13 and later), asked at run time only when you switch on an alert, after BattScope explains why. If you decline, alerts stay inside the app.
  • Foreground service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE): a recording you started keeps running, with its notification, when you leave the app. It stops when the charger is unplugged, the battery is full, you tap Stop, or after 12 hours (charging) or 24 hours (discharge). There is no always-on service.
  • Internet (INTERNET): to load ads, and for nothing else. Your battery data is never sent.
  • Added by the Google libraries: the advertising ID and Android’s privacy-preserving ad APIs (AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS) and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE for ads; and WAKE_LOCK and RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, which Android’s WorkManager uses to run the short battery check about every 15 minutes and to keep it scheduled after a restart.

BattScope asks for no location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage, phone, SMS, Bluetooth, usage-access or battery-statistics permission.

Data retention and deletion

Data stored locally by BattScope (sessions, battery history, alert settings and recent alerts, the names you gave chargers and cables, your preferences, exported files and the diagnostic log) stays on your device and is retained until you remove it, except battery-history points, which are removed after 90 days. BattScope never deletes your sessions on its own.

You can delete this data at any time by:

  • uninstalling the app, which removes all of its local storage, or
  • using Settings → Clear history (sessions and battery history) or Clear all local data (sessions, history and settings), or deleting a single session.

Mobile Castle does not hold a copy of this data, so there is no separate server-side deletion request to make to us.

For data processed by the third parties above (for example Google’s advertising data), please use the controls those parties provide (see their policies linked above) and your device’s Ads settings.

Security

Because BattScope keeps your data on your own device and does not run a Mobile Castle server, your data is not exposed on our infrastructure. A CSV file leaves your device only when you share it.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure; while we aim to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.
