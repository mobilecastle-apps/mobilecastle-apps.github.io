Paintilo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Paintilo is a colouring and drawing app made by Mobile Castle. This policy covers the Paintilo Android app, package com.mobilecastle.paintilo.

This page says the same thing the app itself says in Settings → Privacy, because a policy that differs from the app is worse than no policy. If the two ever disagree, the app is the thing to trust and this page is the thing to fix.

The short answer

Paintilo collects nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no profile and no analytics. The child’s part of the app does not use the internet at all.

Everything below is detail on that.

What Paintilo never asks for

Paintilo never asks for a name, an email address, a birthday, a photo or a password, and there is nothing to sign in to. It does not keep a profile of your child, because it has never been told anything about them.

There are no child profiles, no accounts and no way to create one.

The child’s part of the app is offline

Choosing a picture, colouring it, the guided drawing steps and the saved gallery all run on the device alone. Nothing is sent anywhere and nothing is fetched.

On Android, the released app is built without the internet permission at all, so it could not reach the network even if a future mistake asked it to. That is a property of the installed app rather than a promise about it: a technically minded parent can verify it from the app’s own permission list, which shows no permissions.

Artwork stays on the device

Saved colourings are written into Paintilo’s own private folder on the device. They are never uploaded, and Paintilo has no cloud storage to upload them to.

Paintilo also switches off automatic cloud backup and device-to-device transfer for its own data, so a child’s artwork does not travel to the cloud with a phone backup or onto the next phone. The other side of that is worth saying plainly: if the device is lost or wiped, the artwork is gone, because there is no copy anywhere else.

What is stored on the device, and nothing else

Only these, all on the device, none of them leaving it:

What Why
Saved colourings So a child can come back to their work
Favourites So a child can find a picture again
A grown-up’s settings Difficulty, sound, reduced motion, toolbar side, language

Favourites are a shelf, not a record. If your child taps the heart on a picture, Paintilo remembers that picture on the device so they can find it again. It is never counted, ranked or scored anywhere, and tapping the heart a second time forgets it.

No analytics, no tracking, no advertising

There is no analytics, no tracking and no advertising code in Paintilo. It does not record what your child taps, how long they play, or how often they open it.

There is nothing to buy inside the app.

Children

Paintilo is designed for young children. It is built so that there is nothing to collect about them in the first place, which is a stronger protection than a promise to handle data carefully.

Because Paintilo collects no personal information from anyone, there is no personal information for a parent to review, correct or ask us to delete. If you want to remove everything Paintilo has stored, uninstalling the app removes all of it, and the parent area also offers a “delete everything” control that erases saved artwork, favourites and settings on the spot.

Changes to this policy

If Paintilo ever changes in a way that makes any statement above untrue, this page will be updated in the same release that changes it, not afterwards. The date at the top says when it was last correct.

In particular: if advertising is ever added to Paintilo, the sections above about advertising, tracking and the internet permission would stop being true, and this policy would be rewritten before that version is published. As of the date above, no such version exists.
