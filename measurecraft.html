MeasureCraft — Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for MeasureCraft

App: MeasureCraft: Measure Tools Package name: com.mobilecastle.measurecraft Publisher: MobileCastle (Mobile Castle) Effective date: 14 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the MeasureCraft app ("MeasureCraft", "the app", "we", "us") does and does not handle, and how. Please read it together with the policies of the third parties named below.

By using MeasureCraft you agree to the practices described here.

Summary (plain language)

  • MeasureCraft has no user accounts and no sign-up. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or any similar personal detail.
  • Your measurements stay on your device. The tools run on your phone or tablet, and your saved measurements, projects, calibrations and notes are stored only there; an export leaves your device only when you share it. MobileCastle does not operate any server that receives them.
  • The sound meter never records. It works out the sound level from the microphone as it happens; no audio is saved or sent anywhere.
  • The camera is used only by AR measure, through Google Play Services for AR (ARCore). MeasureCraft does not save or send camera pictures.
  • Your location is used only by the GPS tools, while they are open. A position is stored only when you save a GPS measurement or project, and only on your device.
  • MeasureCraft shows ads through Google AdMob, a third party. To serve ads, AdMob may collect device information and an advertising identifier. Buying the one-time MeasureCraft Pro upgrade removes ads — and for Pro users the ads software is not started at all.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play Billing. The app never sees your card or bank details.

The rest of this policy gives the detail behind that summary.

Information MeasureCraft itself collects

MeasureCraft does not collect personal data on its own servers, because MobileCastle does not run a server that receives your data. Specifically:

  • No account / identity data. There is no login, so we do not collect a name, email address, password, phone number, or profile.
  • No upload of measurements. Readings, saved measurements, projects, locations, notes and exports are not copied to us.
  • No analytics SDK operated by us. MeasureCraft does not embed our own analytics or tracking service. (Google AdMob, Google Play Services for AR and the other Google services described below are independent third parties with their own data handling.)

Data that stays on your device

Some information is stored locally on your phone so the app can work and remember your settings. This data is not transmitted to MobileCastle. It includes:

  • Saved measurements — when you press Save: the tool, its mode, the value and the details shown with it, the confidence label, the calibration profile in use, the date, and any note you type. They are saved in a database in the app's own private storage.
  • Projects — a GPS path or area you walk and save keeps its points (latitude, longitude, altitude, accuracy and time); an AR project keeps its measured points. Projects are stored only in the app's private storage.
  • Calibrations, presets and custom units — the calibration values you set for each tool, your angle presets and your own units.
  • Your preferences — for example theme, language, units, favourite and recently used tools.
  • Exports you create — a CSV or PDF export is written to the app's private cache only so you can share it, and is deleted after a day.
  • A local diagnostic log — MeasureCraft writes a technical log (which tools opened and closed, errors) to a file in the app's own folder on your device, to help troubleshoot problems on real hardware. It never contains your measurements or locations, and it stays on your device unless you deliberately share it.

All of it is removed when you uninstall MeasureCraft. Settings → Clear history removes your saved measurements and projects, and Clear all local data also removes calibrations, presets, custom units and settings. See Data retention and deletion below.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

MeasureCraft is free and supported by ads served through Google AdMob, a Google service. Ads appear only while you are on the free tier, and never on a tool while it is measuring; the MeasureCraft Pro one-time purchase removes ads.

To select and show ads, Google AdMob (and the Google Mobile Ads SDK included in the app) may collect and process information such as:

  • a device advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID),
  • device and network information (for example device type, operating-system version, general/coarse location inferred from your IP address, and similar technical data),
  • ad-interaction and app-usage data related to the ads shown,
  • crash and diagnostic information about the ads software itself.

Google uses this information to serve and measure ads and to help prevent fraud and abuse, in accordance with Google's own policies. Where required by law (for example, for users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), MeasureCraft uses Google's User Messaging Platform to show a consent form before serving personalised ads, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options. If you own MeasureCraft Pro, neither the consent form nor the Google Mobile Ads SDK is started. On the free tier you may also choose to watch a short rewarded ad to unlock one export or one AR measuring session; this is optional.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • AdMob / Google Ads policies: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

You can limit ad personalisation and reset your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads on most Android phones).

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The one-time MeasureCraft Pro upgrade (which unlocks premium features and removes ads) is sold and processed through Google Play Billing. When you buy MeasureCraft Pro:

  • Google handles the payment. MeasureCraft never receives or stores your payment card or bank details.
  • The app only learns whether you own the MeasureCraft Pro entitlement, so it can unlock the features and stop showing ads.

Your purchase is governed by Google Play's terms and privacy policy:

  • Google Play Terms of Service: https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Augmented reality (Google Play Services for AR)

This application runs on Google Play Services for AR (ARCore), which is provided by Google LLC and governed by the Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

AR measure uses the camera only while that tool is open. ARCore processes the camera images on your device to find surfaces and track movement; MeasureCraft keeps only the points you place and the distances it works out from them, and never saves or sends a picture. If your device needs Google Play Services for AR, the app opens its Google Play page; you choose whether to install it.

Tools and what they touch

Each tool reads only what it needs from your device, on your device, and only while it is open. Every permission is requested only when you open the tool that needs it, and the rest of the app keeps working if you say no.

  • Motion and orientation sensors (accelerometer, gyroscope, gravity, rotation) for the level, inclinometer, accelerometer, vibration and motion tools.
  • Magnetometer for the compass and the magnetic field meter.
  • Light, pressure, temperature and humidity sensors, where your device has them, for the environment tools.
  • Microphone for the sound meter: it measures the sound level as it happens. No audio is recorded, saved or sent.
  • Camera for AR measure, through ARCore (see above).
  • Location for GPS distance & area and GPS speed: positions are shown while the tool is open and stored only if you save a measurement or project.
  • Screen size and density for the on-screen ruler.

App permissions and why they are needed

MeasureCraft requests the following Android permissions. Each is used only for the feature described:

  • Camera (CAMERA) — for AR measure.
  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) — for the sound meter.
  • Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) — for the GPS tools. Only while the app is open; there is no background location. You may allow approximate location only; the GPS tools then say their results are less exact.
  • Network state (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) — to check whether you are online before loading an ad.
  • Internet (INTERNET) — to load ads, to reach Google Play for the Pro purchase, and for Google Play Services for AR. Measurements are not sent.
  • Added by the Google libraries — the advertising ID and Android's privacy-preserving ad APIs (AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS) for ads on the free tier; Google Play purchases (BILLING); and background-work permissions (WAKE_LOCK, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, FOREGROUND_SERVICE) that the ads library uses for its own short tasks.

MeasureCraft asks for no storage, contacts, phone, SMS or background location permission.

Because the app shows ads, it follows Google Play's Families and ads policies for a general-audience app. If you believe a child has used the app in a way that raises a concern, please contact us using the details below.

Data retention and deletion

Data stored locally by MeasureCraft (saved measurements, projects, notes, calibrations, presets, custom units, preferences, exports and the diagnostic log) stays on your device and is retained until you remove it. MeasureCraft never deletes your measurements on its own: on the free tier, measurements beyond the latest five are locked, not deleted.

You can delete this data at any time by:

  • uninstalling the app, which removes all of its local storage, or
  • using Settings → Clear history (measurements and projects) or Clear all local data (also calibrations, presets, custom units and settings), or deleting a single measurement or project from its page.

MobileCastle does not hold a copy of this data, so there is no separate server-side deletion request to make to us.

For data processed by the third parties above (for example Google's advertising data), please use the controls those parties provide — see their policies linked above, and your device's Ads settings.

Security

Because MeasureCraft keeps your data on your own device and does not run a MobileCastle server, your measurements and locations are not exposed on our infrastructure. Exports leave your device only when you share them.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure; while we aim to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.
