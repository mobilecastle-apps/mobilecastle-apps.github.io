MeasureCraft — Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for MeasureCraft

App: MeasureCraft: Measure Tools Package name: com.mobilecastle.measurecraft Publisher: Mobile Castle Effective date: 3 November 2026

This policy is for the free version of MeasureCraft, which offers no in-app purchases.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the MeasureCraft app (“MeasureCraft”, “the app”, “we”, “us”) does and does not handle, and how. Please read it together with the policies of the third parties named below.

By using MeasureCraft you agree to the practices described here.

Summary (plain language)

  • MeasureCraft has no user accounts and no sign-up. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or any similar personal detail.
  • Your measurements stay on your device. The tools run on your phone or tablet, and what you save (measurements, notes, calibrations) is stored only there; an export leaves your device only when you share it. Mobile Castle does not operate any server that receives your data.
  • The sound meter never records. It works out the sound level from the microphone as it happens; no audio is saved or sent anywhere.
  • The camera is used only by AR measure, through Google Play Services for AR (ARCore), a Google service. MeasureCraft does not save or send camera pictures.
  • Your location is used only by the GPS tools, while they are open. This version never stores a position: a saved GPS measurement keeps the distance, area or speed and its accuracy, not where you were.
  • MeasureCraft shows ads through Google AdMob, a third party. To serve ads, AdMob may collect device information and an advertising identifier. Ads never receive your measurements, locations, notes or exports.
  • No purchases in this version. MeasureCraft offers nothing to buy: it does not connect to Google Play Billing, and it does not ask for the billing permission. Premium features are coming soon.

The rest of this policy gives the detail behind that summary.

Information MeasureCraft itself collects

MeasureCraft does not collect personal data on its own servers, because Mobile Castle does not run a server that receives your data. Specifically:

  • No account / identity data. There is no login, so we do not collect a name, email address, password, phone number, or profile.
  • No upload of measurements. Readings, saved measurements, locations, notes and exports are not copied to us.
  • No analytics SDK operated by us. MeasureCraft does not embed our own analytics, crash-reporting or tracking service. (Google AdMob, Google Play Services for AR and the other Google services described below are independent third parties with their own data handling.)

Data that stays on your device

Some information is stored locally on your device so the app can work and remember your settings. This data is not transmitted to Mobile Castle, and it is excluded from Android’s cloud backup. It includes:

  • Saved measurements: when you tap Save, the tool, its mode, the value and the details shown with it (for example minimum, maximum and average, or the accuracy GPS reported), the confidence label, the calibration in use, the date, and any note you type. They are saved in a database in the app’s own private storage. This version shows the latest five measurements; older ones stay on your device, locked, and are never deleted on their own.
  • No projects in this version: saving a walked path or area with its points under a name is a planned Premium feature. A saved GPS measurement keeps the distance, area or perimeter, speed and accuracy, never a position.
  • Calibrations: the calibration values you set for each tool, with the date.
  • Your preferences: for example theme, language, units, favorite and recently used tools.
  • Rewarded-ad unlocks: how many exports or AR sessions you have unlocked with a rewarded ad and not yet used (a number, nothing else).
  • Exports you create: a CSV or PDF export is written to the app’s private cache only so you can share it; exports older than a day are deleted the next time one is made.
  • A local diagnostic log: MeasureCraft writes a technical log (which tools opened and closed, errors) to a file in the app’s own folder on your device, to help troubleshoot problems on real hardware. It never contains your measurements, locations or notes, and it stays on your device unless you deliberately share it.

All of it is removed when you uninstall MeasureCraft. Settings → Clear history removes your saved measurements, and Clear all local data also removes calibrations, settings and exports. See Data retention and deletion below.

Tools and what they touch

Each tool reads only what it needs from your device, on your device, and only while it is open. Every permission is requested only when you open the tool that needs it, and the rest of the app keeps working if you say no.

  • Motion and orientation sensors (accelerometer, gyroscope, gravity, rotation) for the bubble level, the inclinometer and the motion tools.
  • Magnetometer for the compass and the magnetic field meter.
  • Light, pressure, temperature and humidity sensors, where your device has them, for the environment tools. Temperature is never estimated from the battery.
  • Microphone for the sound meter: it measures the sound level as it happens. No audio is recorded, saved or sent.
  • Camera for AR measure, through Google Play Services for AR (see below).
  • Location for GPS distance & area and GPS speed: positions are used while the tool is open. You may allow approximate location only; the GPS tools then say their results are less exact.
  • Screen size and density for the on-screen ruler and protractor.

Augmented reality (Google Play Services for AR)

This application runs on Google Play Services for AR (ARCore), which is provided by Google LLC and governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google Play Services for AR handles your data: https://support.google.com/ar?p=how-google-play-services-for-ar-handles-your-data

AR measure uses the camera only while that tool is open. ARCore processes the camera images on your device to find surfaces and track movement; MeasureCraft keeps only the points you place and the distances and areas it works out from them, and never saves or sends a picture. In this version, AR measure opens for one session after a short rewarded ad you choose to watch (see Advertising below). If your device needs Google Play Services for AR, the app opens its Google Play page; you choose whether to install it.

According to Google, while an app uses it, Google Play Services for AR collects:

  • identifiers: Google account identifiers if you are signed in to a Google account, otherwise device identifiers;
  • performance and diagnostic data about the app, the ARCore software and your device (for example battery life, loading time, latency and frame rate);
  • data about the ARCore API calls the app makes.

Google states that this data is encrypted in transit, is used for analytics and to improve AR experiences, and is not shared with third parties. MeasureCraft does not use ARCore’s Cloud Anchors or Geospatial API, so the location and video data those features would involve are not collected.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

MeasureCraft is free and supported by ads served through Google AdMob, a Google service. Ads appear as banners on the tool list, the History and Settings screens, the Calibration Center and saved results, never on a tool while it measures, in the AR camera view, while the sound meter listens, during a calibration or on Android’s permission prompt. There are no full-screen ads when you open the app or move between screens.

The ad requests MeasureCraft sends carry no content from the app: no measurement, location, note or export is ever passed to AdMob. To select and show ads, Google AdMob (and the Google Mobile Ads SDK included in the app) may collect and process information such as:

  • a device advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID),
  • device and network information (for example device type, operating-system version, approximate location inferred from your IP address, and similar technical data),
  • ad-interaction and app-usage data related to the ads shown,
  • crash and diagnostic information about the ads software itself.

Google uses this information to serve and measure ads and to help prevent fraud and abuse, in accordance with Google’s own policies. Where required by law (for example, for users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), MeasureCraft uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to show a consent form before serving personalised ads, and no ad is requested until that choice is made; you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options. You may also choose to watch a short rewarded ad to unlock one CSV or PDF export, or one AR measuring session; this is optional, and the app says what the ad unlocks before it plays.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • AdMob / Google Ads policies: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

You can limit ad personalisation and reset your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads on most Android phones).

Features planned for a future Premium version show “Premium features are coming soon.” If purchases are added in a later version, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

App permissions and why they are needed

MeasureCraft requests the following Android permissions. Each is used only for the feature described:

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): for the sound meter.
  • Camera (CAMERA): for AR measure.
  • Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): for the GPS tools. Only while the app is open; there is no background location.
  • Network state (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): to check whether you are online before loading an ad.
  • Internet (INTERNET): to load ads and for Google Play Services for AR. Measurements are not sent.
  • Added by the Google libraries: the advertising ID and Android’s privacy-preserving ad APIs (AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS) for ads; and background-work permissions (WAKE_LOCK, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, FOREGROUND_SERVICE) that the Google libraries use for their own short tasks.

MeasureCraft asks for no storage, contacts, accounts, phone, SMS, call log or background-location permission. Haptic feedback needs no permission.

Data retention and deletion

Data stored locally by MeasureCraft (saved measurements, notes, calibrations, preferences, exports and the diagnostic log) stays on your device and is retained until you remove it. MeasureCraft never deletes your measurements on its own: this version shows the latest five measurements and keeps the rest on your device, locked, not deleted.

You can delete this data at any time by:

  • uninstalling the app, which removes all of its local storage, or
  • using Settings → Clear history (your saved measurements) or Clear all local data (also calibrations, settings and exports), or deleting a single measurement from its page.

Mobile Castle does not hold a copy of this data, so there is no separate server-side deletion request to make to us.

For data processed by the third parties above (for example Google’s advertising data), please use the controls those parties provide (see their policies linked above) and your device’s Ads settings.

Security

Because MeasureCraft keeps your data on your own device and does not run a Mobile Castle server, your measurements and locations are not exposed on our infrastructure. Exports leave your device only when you share them.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure; while we aim to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.
