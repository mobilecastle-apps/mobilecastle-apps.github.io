Castora — Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for Castora

App: Castora Package name: com.mobilecastle.castora Publisher: Mobile Castle Effective date: 18 November 2026

This policy is for Castora as published on Google Play, which offers the optional one-time Castora Pro purchase.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Castora app ("Castora", "the app", "we", "us") does and does not handle, and how. Please read it together with the policies of the third parties named below.

By using Castora you agree to the practices described here.

Summary (plain language)

  • Castora has no user accounts and no sign-up. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or any similar personal detail.
  • Your videos, music and photos never leave your device or your local network. When you cast, the app streams them directly from your phone to the TV or streaming device you choose, over your own Wi‑Fi. Nothing is uploaded to Mobile Castle: Mobile Castle does not operate any content server for your media.
  • If you use the IPTV feature, the login details or playlist you enter are stored only on your device and are sent only to the IPTV provider you chose, so the app can play your streams. They are never sent to Mobile Castle. If your provider's server does not answer as it should, the app asks Google's connectivity check whether your internet works, to tell you which of the two is the problem; that request sends nothing about you.
  • Castora deletes a video from your device only when you ask it to, by ticking Also delete from this device. For a video another app saved, Android asks for your consent (on Android 10 and earlier, once, as its storage permission). Nothing about it leaves your device.
  • On phones, Castora shows ads through Google AdMob, a third party, on the free tier. To serve ads, AdMob may collect device information and an advertising identifier. Buying the one-time Castora Pro upgrade removes ads, and for Pro users the ads software is not started at all. Castora shows no ads on Android TV.
  • On the free tier, the phone app also shows Mobile Castle's own promotions of its other apps. To have them ready, it downloads a catalogue of those apps and their pictures from mobilecastle-apps.github.io; no personal data is sent.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play Billing. The app never sees your card or bank details.

The rest of this policy gives the detail behind that summary.

Information Castora itself collects

Castora does not collect personal data on its own servers, because Mobile Castle does not run a server that receives your data. Specifically:

  • No account / identity data. There is no login, so we do not collect a name, email address, password, phone number, or profile.
  • No content upload. Local videos, music and photos you cast, and IPTV streams you play or record, are not copied to us.
  • No analytics SDK operated by us. Castora does not embed our own analytics or tracking service. (Google AdMob and the Google services described below are independent third parties with their own data handling.)

Data that stays on your device

Some information is stored locally on your device so the app can work and remember your settings. This data is not transmitted to Mobile Castle. It includes:

  • IPTV sources you add: for an Xtream login, the server URL, username, and password; for a playlist, the M3U URL or the path to a local playlist file. These are saved in the app's local settings storage (Android SharedPreferences) and, for large catalogs, a cache file inside the app's own private storage. They are used only to fetch and play your streams, and are sent only to the IPTV provider you entered.
  • Your preferences and library state, for example favourites, playlists, Continue watching, the last tab you had open, sort order, and watch progress.
  • Downloaded and recorded videos (IPTV downloads and live-channel recordings), saved on the device in the Movies/Castora folder, where you can also see, play and delete them with other apps.
  • Your Castora Pro status. Castora keeps a record of whether you own Castora Pro, so Pro works without an internet connection. The record is sealed to your device with Android's app-specific device ID, so a copied or edited record is not accepted; the ID is used only for that check on the device and is never stored in the record or sent anywhere.
  • Mobile Castle's catalogue: the list of Mobile Castle's apps and their pictures, downloaded for the promotions described below. It holds nothing about you.
  • A local diagnostic log. Castora can write a technical casting log to a file in the app's own storage to help troubleshoot casting problems on real hardware. This log stays on your device unless you deliberately share it, and the app is designed to keep sensitive values (such as IPTV credentials) out of it.

The settings, sources, caches, catalogue and log live in the app's private storage: they are removed when you uninstall Castora, or when you use the app's in-app actions to remove a source or clear a cache. Downloads and recordings in Movies/Castora are your files: they stay until you delete them (in the app, or with any file manager), including after an uninstall. See Data retention and deletion below.

Deleting videos from your device

When you remove a video from a playlist or from Continue watching, or delete a whole playlist, Castora offers a box, Also delete from this device. It is never ticked for you. Only when you tick it does Castora delete the video file itself from your device, not just the entry in the app:

  • Castora's own files (its downloads and recordings) are deleted directly.
  • A video another app saved is deleted through Android: on Android 11 and later, Android shows its own dialog and deletes the video only if you allow it. On Android 10 and earlier, Castora first asks for Android's storage permission if it does not have it yet (see WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE below), and Android 10 may also ask you to confirm the deletion.
  • Castora never deletes the video that is playing or the one that plays next, and it never deletes anything on its own.

Deleting happens on your device only. Nothing about the video, or about what you delete, is sent to Mobile Castle or anyone else.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Castora is free and supported by ads served through Google AdMob, a Google service. Ads appear only on phones and tablets, and only while you are on the free tier; the Castora Pro one-time purchase removes ads. Castora does not start the ads software on Android TV.

To select and show ads, Google AdMob (and the Google Mobile Ads SDK included in the app) may collect and process information such as:

  • a device advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID), and similar device identifiers;
  • your IP address, from which Google infers your approximate location, together with device and network information (for example device type and operating-system version);
  • app interactions related to the ads shown (for example app launches, taps and ad views);
  • crash logs and diagnostic information about the ads software itself.

Google uses this information to serve and measure ads, for analytics, and to help prevent fraud and abuse, in accordance with Google's own policies. These are the data types Castora declares in the Data safety section of its Google Play listing. Ads never receive your videos, music, photos, IPTV details or watch history.

Where required by law (for example, for users in the European Economic Area), Castora uses Google's User Messaging Platform to show a consent form before serving personalised ads. If you own Castora Pro, neither the consent form nor the Google Mobile Ads SDK is started. On the free tier you may also choose to watch a short rewarded ad, for example for two ad-free hours or to fix a video's sound; this is always optional.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • AdMob / Google Ads policies: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

You can limit ad personalisation and reset your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Security & privacy → Ads, depending on your phone).

Mobile Castle's own promotions and More apps

On the free tier, Castora's phone app also shows promotions for Mobile Castle's other apps: a banner where a Google ad cannot be shown, and, when you choose to watch an ad, sometimes a short full-screen Mobile Castle promotion in place of Google's, for example when Google has none for you; it gives the same reward once it has played to the end, never for closing it early or for installing anything. Each promotion is marked "Mobile Castle". Tapping one opens that app's page on Google Play; the link tells Google Play only that the visit came from a promotion in Castora, nothing about you.

The phone app's Settings also has a More apps from Mobile Castle row, for everyone, which opens Mobile Castle's developer page on Google Play. On Android TV, Settings shows the same page as a QR code, drawn by the app itself; nothing is downloaded for it.

To have the promotions ready, offline too, the phone app downloads a catalogue of Mobile Castle's apps from Mobile Castle's website, https://mobilecastle-apps.github.io/promos.json, and the pictures it names from https://mobilecastle-apps.github.io/promos/. It checks the catalogue at most once a day, when the app starts or when you return to it, over a secure (https) connection, and downloads only what has changed.

  • No personal data is sent. The request carries no identifier, account, setting or usage data, and nothing about your videos or IPTV sources. Like any web request, it reaches the site with your device's IP address and the standard technical details Android adds to every request (such as the Android version and device model). The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which, like any web server, sees these when it delivers the files (GitHub's privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Mobile Castle receives no record of these requests.
  • The catalogue and pictures are kept in the app's own storage and removed when you uninstall Castora.
  • Promotions are not personalised. To leave out an app you already have, Castora checks on your device which Mobile Castle apps are installed; that check never leaves your device.
  • If you own Castora Pro, no promotion is shown and no pictures are downloaded for one. The app still checks the small catalogue file once a day; the More apps row stays.

When your IPTV server does not answer

When Castora cannot reach your IPTV provider's server, or the server answers with something that is not an IPTV catalogue, the app checks whether your internet connection itself works, so it can tell you whether to check your connection or to ask your provider for the server's current address. For this it asks Google's connectivity check, http://connectivitycheck.gstatic.com/generate_204, the same address Android itself uses for this check, and looks only at whether Google sends back its empty answer.

  • It happens only after a request to your IPTV server has failed, never in the background and never for anything else.
  • No personal data is sent. The request carries no identifier, account, setting or usage data, and nothing about your videos or IPTV sources; it does not name your provider. Like any web request, it reaches Google with your device's IP address and the standard technical details Android adds to every request. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Nothing about it is stored, and Mobile Castle receives no record of it.

Casting and local streaming (Google Cast, DLNA, Roku)

Castora casts your local videos, music, photos and IPTV streams to a TV or streaming device on your own local network. To do this the app:

  • discovers compatible devices on your Wi‑Fi (Google Cast / Chromecast and Android TV, DLNA renderers, and Roku devices),
  • runs a small local media server on your phone and sends the chosen device a local-network URL (using your phone's Wi‑Fi IP address) so it can fetch the file directly from your phone.

Music and photos are chosen with Android's own system picker, so Castora needs no permission to read your music or photo library; it only receives the files you pick. The content is streamed device-to-device over your LAN. It is not routed through, or stored on, any Mobile Castle server. Castora uses Google's built-in Cast receiver and does not run a custom cloud receiver for your content.

The Google Cast SDK is a Google component; its use of information is covered by Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

App permissions and why they are needed

Castora requests the following Android permissions. Each is used only for the feature described:

  • Internet (INTERNET): to run the local media server, discover cast devices, fetch and stream your IPTV content from your provider, load ads, reach Google Play for the Pro purchase, download Mobile Castle's catalogue for its promotions, and check whether your internet works when an IPTV server does not answer.
  • Network state (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): to check whether you are connected and adapt behaviour (for example when a connection drops).
  • Wi‑Fi state (ACCESS_WIFI_STATE): to determine your phone's Wi‑Fi IP address so the cast device can reach the local media server.
  • Wi‑Fi multicast (CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE): while a TV plays a video from your phone, to let the TV's announcements through with the screen off, so a dropped cast can find the TV again.
  • Keep awake (WAKE_LOCK): to keep the phone from sleeping while it is actively casting, so playback isn't interrupted.
  • Foreground service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK): to keep the media server and cast session alive while the app is in the background during playback.
  • Foreground service, data sync (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC): to let a long IPTV download, or a live-channel recording you started, continue if you leave the app.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): on Android 13+, to show the ongoing notification required while a casting session or download runs in the background.
  • Read video (READ_MEDIA_VIDEO on Android 13+, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on Android 12 and below): to let you pick local video files from your device to cast. Castora reads the videos you choose; it does not upload them. When you tick Also delete from this device, Castora deletes that video from your device as described in Deleting videos from your device.
  • Write storage (WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, Android 10 and below only; Android 11 and later never give it to Castora): on Android 9 and below, to save your downloads and recordings into Movies/Castora; on Android 10 and below, to delete a video another app saved, only when you ask Castora to delete it.
  • Added by the Google libraries: the advertising ID and Android's privacy-preserving ad APIs (AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS) for ads, and Google Play purchases (com.android.vending.BILLING) for the Pro purchase.

Castora asks for no contacts, accounts, phone, SMS, call log, camera, microphone, location or all-files-access permission.

Children

Castora is a general-audience media utility and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app is intended for general audiences and, in line with the store listing, is not aimed at or designed to appeal to children.

Because the app shows ads, it follows Google Play's Families and ads policies for a general-audience app. If you believe a child has used the app in a way that raises a concern, please contact us using the details below.

Data retention and deletion

Data stored locally by Castora (IPTV sources, preferences, cached catalogs, Mobile Castle's catalogue, downloads, and the diagnostic log) stays on your device and is retained until you remove it.

You can delete this data at any time by:

  • uninstalling the app, which removes its local storage (downloads and recordings in Movies/Castora stay until you delete them), or
  • using the app's in-app actions to remove an IPTV source, clear cached content, delete a download or recording, or delete a video from your device (Also delete from this device).

Mobile Castle does not hold a copy of this data, so there is no separate server-side deletion request to make to us.

For data processed by the third parties above (for example Google's advertising data), please use the controls those parties provide (see their policies linked above) and your device's Ads settings.

Security

  • Because Castora keeps your data on your own device and does not run a Mobile Castle content server, your videos and IPTV details are not exposed on our infrastructure.
  • Casting happens over your local network; keep your Wi‑Fi network secured.
  • Please note that IPTV streams are often delivered over plain (non-encrypted) http connections, because many IPTV providers do not offer working secure endpoints. This is a limitation of IPTV providers, not of Castora, and applies to traffic between your device and the provider you chose.
  • Mobile Castle's catalogue and pictures are only downloaded over https. The connectivity check uses plain http, as Android's own does; it carries nothing about you.
  • No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure; while we aim to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.
