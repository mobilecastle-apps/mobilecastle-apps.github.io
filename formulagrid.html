FormulaGrid — Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for FormulaGrid

App: FormulaGrid: Calculator Package name: com.mobilecastle.formulagrid Publisher: MobileCastle (Mobile Castle) Effective date: 5 November 2026

This policy is for the version of FormulaGrid that offers the optional one-time FormulaGrid Pro purchase.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the FormulaGrid app (“FormulaGrid”, “the app”, “we”, “us”) does and does not handle, and how. Please read it together with the policies of the third parties named below.

By using FormulaGrid you agree to the practices described here.

Summary (plain language)

  • FormulaGrid has no user accounts and no sign-up. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or any similar personal detail.
  • Your calculations stay on your device. Every calculation runs on your phone or tablet, without the internet, and your history, favorites and saved scenarios are stored only there; a result leaves your device only when you share it. Mobile Castle does not operate any server that receives them.
  • FormulaGrid needs no special permissions: no camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage access.
  • FormulaGrid shows ads through Google AdMob, a third party, on the free tier. To serve ads, AdMob may collect device information and an advertising identifier. Ads never receive your calculations, inputs or results. Buying the one-time FormulaGrid Pro upgrade removes ads, and for Pro users the ads software is not started at all.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play Billing. The app never sees your card or bank details.
  • On the free tier the app also shows Mobile Castle’s own promotions of its other apps. To have them ready, it downloads a catalogue of those apps and their pictures from mobilecastle-apps.github.io; no personal data is sent.

The rest of this policy gives the detail behind that summary.

Information FormulaGrid itself collects

FormulaGrid does not collect personal data on its own servers, because Mobile Castle does not run a server that receives your data. Specifically:

  • No account / identity data. There is no login, so we do not collect a name, email address, password, phone number, or profile.
  • No upload of calculations. The numbers you enter, your results, history and scenarios are not copied to us.
  • No analytics SDK operated by us. FormulaGrid does not embed our own analytics, crash-reporting or tracking service. (Google AdMob and the other Google services described below are independent third parties with their own data handling.)

Data that stays on your device

Some information is stored locally on your device so the app can work and remember your settings. This data is not transmitted to Mobile Castle, and it is excluded from Android’s cloud backup. It includes:

  • History: the calculations you finish: the keypad’s expression and its result, or a calculator’s inputs, the formula version and its result, with the date. They are saved in a database in the app’s own private storage. On the free tier the latest twenty entries are shown; older ones stay on your device, locked, and are never deleted on their own.
  • Favorites, recently used calculators and saved scenarios: the calculators you star or opened last, and the sets of inputs you name and save.
  • Your own formulas (Pro): the names, formulas and named values you write.
  • Your Pro purchase record: Google Play’s last answer about whether you own FormulaGrid Pro, so Pro is available as soon as the app opens, with or without the internet. It is sealed with a key in the app combined with Android’s app-specific device ID, so a copied or edited record is not accepted. The device ID is used only for that seal: it is not stored, not sent anywhere, and it is not the advertising identifier.
  • Your preferences: for example theme and your custom colour themes, language, units, currency symbol, thousands separators, haptic feedback, the angle unit, the keypad layout, the last inputs of each calculator, and the keypad’s current expression and memory.
  • A local diagnostic log: FormulaGrid writes a technical log (which features started, errors) to a file in the app’s own folder on your device, to help troubleshoot problems on real hardware. It never contains your calculations, inputs or results, and it stays on your device unless you deliberately share it.
  • Mobile Castle’s catalogue: the list of Mobile Castle’s apps and their pictures, downloaded for the promotions described below. It holds nothing about you.

All of it is removed when you uninstall FormulaGrid. Settings → Clear history removes your history, and Clear all local data removes history, scenarios, formulas, favorites and settings. See Data retention and deletion below.

When you share a result or a table, Android’s share sheet sends it, as text, to the app you choose (for example a messaging app). What that app does with it is governed by its own privacy policy.

Backups and exports

FormulaGrid Pro can save a backup (favorites, history, scenarios and formulas) or a CSV file of your history. Android’s own “save as” screen opens and you choose where the file goes; the app writes only that file and needs no storage permission. Restoring opens a file you pick in the same way and adds its content to the app. A file you save stays where you put it, under your control; Mobile Castle never receives it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

FormulaGrid is free and supported by ads served through Google AdMob, a Google service. Ads appear only while you are on the free tier, as banners in the Library and History lists and below full result tables (for example the complete amortization schedule), never on the calculator keypad, next to the fields you type in, in the formula editor or on an advanced calculator’s offer screen. There are no full-screen ads when you open the app or move between screens. The FormulaGrid Pro one-time purchase removes ads.

The ad requests FormulaGrid sends carry no content from the app: no calculation, input, result, scenario or formula is ever passed to AdMob. To select and show ads, Google AdMob (and the Google Mobile Ads SDK included in the app) may collect and process information such as:

  • a device advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID),
  • device and network information (for example device type, operating-system version, approximate location inferred from your IP address, and similar technical data),
  • ad-interaction and app-usage data related to the ads shown,
  • crash and diagnostic information about the ads software itself.

Google uses this information to serve and measure ads and to help prevent fraud and abuse, in accordance with Google’s own policies. Where required by law (for example, for users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), FormulaGrid uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to show a consent form before serving personalised ads, and no ad is requested until that choice is made; you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options. If you own FormulaGrid Pro, neither the consent form nor the Google Mobile Ads SDK is started. On the free tier you may also choose to watch a short rewarded ad to use one advanced calculator until you close the app; this is optional, and the app says what the ad unlocks before it plays.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • AdMob / Google Ads policies: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

You can limit ad personalisation and reset your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads on most Android phones).

Mobile Castle’s own promotions

On the free tier, FormulaGrid also shows promotions for Mobile Castle’s other apps: a banner where a Google ad cannot be shown, and, if you choose to watch one, a short full-screen promotion in place of a rewarded ad; it unlocks the same advanced calculator until you close the app when it has played to the end. Settings also has a More apps from Mobile Castle row, for everyone, which opens Mobile Castle’s page on Google Play. Tapping a promotion opens that app’s page on Google Play.

To have these promotions ready, offline too, the app downloads a catalogue of Mobile Castle’s apps and their pictures from Mobile Castle’s website, https://mobilecastle-apps.github.io, when it starts and when you return to it. No personal data is sent: nothing about you, your device or your calculations goes with the request. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which, like any web server, sees your device’s IP address when it delivers the files (GitHub’s privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). The catalogue and pictures are kept in the app’s own storage and removed when you uninstall FormulaGrid.

Promotions are not personalised. To leave out an app you already have, FormulaGrid checks on your device which Mobile Castle apps are installed; that check never leaves your device. If you own FormulaGrid Pro, no promotion is shown and no pictures are downloaded for one; the More apps row stays.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The one-time FormulaGrid Pro upgrade (which removes ads and unlocks unlimited history, backup and export, the advanced calculators, your own formulas, custom colours and the keypad layouts) is sold and processed through Google Play Billing. When you buy FormulaGrid Pro:

  • Google handles the payment. FormulaGrid never receives or stores your payment card or bank details.
  • The app only learns whether you own the FormulaGrid Pro entitlement, so it can unlock the features and stop showing ads. It checks this with Google Play quietly in the background, when the app starts and when you return to it; Pro keeps working when you are offline.

Your purchase is governed by Google Play’s terms and privacy policy:

  • Google Play Terms of Service: https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

App permissions and why they are needed

FormulaGrid requests the following Android permissions. Each is used only for the feature described:

  • Internet (INTERNET): to load ads, to reach Google Play for the Pro purchase, and to download Mobile Castle’s catalogue for its promotions. Calculations are not sent.
  • Network state (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): to check whether you are online before loading an ad.
  • Added by the Google libraries: the advertising ID and Android’s privacy-preserving ad APIs (AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS) for ads; Google Play purchases (com.android.vending.BILLING), added by the Play Billing library; and background-work permissions (WAKE_LOCK, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, FOREGROUND_SERVICE) that the Google libraries use for their own short tasks.

FormulaGrid asks for no camera, microphone, location, storage, contacts, phone or SMS permission. Saving and opening a backup or export goes through Android’s own screens, which need no permission.

Children

FormulaGrid is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app is intended for general audiences and, in line with the store listing, is not aimed at or designed to appeal to children.

Because the app shows ads, it follows Google Play’s Families and ads policies for a general-audience app. If you believe a child has used the app in a way that raises a concern, please contact us using the details below.

Data retention and deletion

Data stored locally by FormulaGrid (history, favorites, scenarios, formulas, preferences, the Pro purchase record and the diagnostic log) stays on your device and is retained until you remove it. FormulaGrid never deletes your history on its own: on the free tier, entries beyond the latest twenty are locked, not deleted.

You can delete this data at any time by:

  • uninstalling the app, which removes all of its local storage, or
  • using Settings → Clear history (all history) or Clear all local data (history, scenarios, formulas, favorites and settings), or deleting a single history entry or scenario.

Clear all local data keeps your Pro purchase record, because what you bought is not local data to wipe; the purchase itself belongs to your Google Play account. Uninstalling removes the record too, and Pro comes back the next time Google Play answers (or with Restore purchase).

Mobile Castle does not hold a copy of this data, so there is no separate server-side deletion request to make to us. Backup and export files you saved elsewhere stay where you put them until you delete them.

For data processed by the third parties above (for example Google’s advertising data), please use the controls those parties provide (see their policies linked above) and your device’s Ads settings.

Security

Because FormulaGrid keeps your data on your own device and does not run a Mobile Castle server, your calculations are not exposed on our infrastructure. They leave your device only when you share them or save a backup or export.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure; while we aim to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.
